Who this is for

Owners and teams at small and mid-size companies that run on spreadsheets and have outgrown them. If someone on your team spends hours each week copying, pasting and reformatting, that job can usually be automated.

I work with clients across India and the UAE. I am based in Bengaluru, and the UAE is only 1.5 hours behind, so calls are easy to schedule.

Jobs that suit this well

  • Monthly MIS packs for management
  • Sales and pipeline trackers
  • Invoice and GST or VAT reconciliation sheets
  • Stock and inventory reports
  • Merging many branch or team files into one
